Gameplay & Graphism

Citlali

Citlali is a small dandelion citizen full of curiosity and determination. Kind and optimistic, she is the first to realize that something terrible has happened to Queen Amann. Refusing to abandon her kingdom, she sets out on a great quest to gather the ingredients needed to bake the magical kouign amann. Her courage, kindness, and energy make her the true heart of the adventure.

Tonatiuh

Tonatiuh is a brave skeleton wearing a large colorful poncho. Behind his intimidating appearance hides a loyal and generous friend, always ready to help those in need. As a proud citizen of the Pastry Kingdom, he immediately agrees to join the adventure to save Queen Amann. With his strength and calm spirit, he protects Clitlali throughout their journey across a world filled with danger… and sweets.
